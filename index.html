About

I am a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK). I conduct research in the Wave Intelligence Lab under the supervision of Prof. Shengchao Liu. I received my bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Tongji University in 2025.

My research focuses on multimodal learning and spatiotemporal perception. In September 2026, my work, Observation-Adaptive Spatiotemporal Positional Encoding: A State-Space Perspective, received First Prize (250k) in AI for Research Algorithmic Problems at the Global Open-source AI Challenge (GOAI) 2026.

I am currently exploring methods for multimodal fusion.
